Tenuki Beta

Tenuki is a new Go app for smartphones and tablets focused on providing a clean, polished experience for playing on OGS, Pandanet, and Fox.

iOS

Join the beta on TestFlight: https://testflight.apple.com/join/TAjGWfMx

Android

  1. Go to https://groups.google.com/g/tenuki-android-beta and click "Join". Make sure you are logged in with the Google account you use for the Play Store.
  2. Next, go to https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.lazerwalker.tenuki and click "Become a Tester".
  3. On your phone, go to https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lazerwalker.tenuki and install the app.
